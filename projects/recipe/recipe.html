Cucumber salad recipe

The Pre Prep

  • Slice Up Cucumbers Into Bite Sized Pieces
  • Salt Cucumbers
  • Let sit so water can come out
  • Squeeze out excess water

Now sauce up your cucumbers!

Use Whatever is available to you
Here's What I use:
  • Sesame oil
  • MSG
  • Soy Sauce
  • Chili Oil
  • Rice Vinegar
  • Gochugaru
  • Minced Garlic
  • A dash Of Sugar
 Measure with your heart!
