Cucumber Salad

Crunchy, salty, spicy, and easy

The Pre Prep

  1. Slice cucumbers into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Salt the cucumbers.
  3. Let them sit so the water can come out.
  4. Squeeze out the excess water.

Now Sauce Up Your Cucumbers!

Use whatever is available to you. Here's what I use:

  • Sesame oil
  • MSG
  • Soy sauce
  • Chili oil
  • Rice vinegar
  • Gochugaru
  • Minced garlic
  • A dash of sugar

Measure with your heart!
