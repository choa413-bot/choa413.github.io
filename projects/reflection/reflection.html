Reflection

What I noticed about my Core 1 Interaction projects

Color

I use a strong vibrant background color in my designs.

My most recurring color would be the teal on my landing page and I used it as a recurring element in the box shadow’s I created for some of my images and boxes.

Type

I usually use DOTO as my title font and Pixelify Sans as the font for my paragraphs and other details.

Visuals

At least one visual element of sorts will show up in my web designs whether it’s a gif, photo, or an animation.

Personality

I like to try to show personality in my websites whether it’s through humorous comments or onomatopoeia’s like “ZZZZZZZ” or “CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH”.

Feeling alive

I wanted my websites to feel alive and have a cohesive environment of sorts.

The animations and interactive elements only make a website feel more lively, like you said once during our class a website is like a canvas.
