Reflection

I use a strong vibrant background color in my designs. I usually use DOTO as my title font and Pixelify Sans as the font for my paragraphs and other details. At least one visual element of sorts will show up in my web designs whether it’s a gif, photo, or an animation.

My most recurring color would be the teal on my landing page, I used as the background color for my web collage page and it’s sprinkled throughout my other webpages as well. I like to try to show personality in my websites whether it’s through humorous comments or onomatopoeia’s like “ZZZZZZZ” or “CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH”.

I wanted my websites to feel alive and have a cohesive environment of sorts. The animations and interactive elements only make a website feel more lively, like you said once during our class a website is like a canvas.
